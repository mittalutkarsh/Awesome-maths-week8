Geometric sequences
HomeNotesLesson

The plan

  1. Default back to a, ar, ar², … Put each known term on its rung. A repeating block is one rung.
  2. Divide one rung by another to get the power of r you know. Don't solve for r unless you must.
  3. Build what you need: a term from that power, or a sum from a, r and the next rung rn+1.
  4. Check: did an even power hide a ±? List the first few rungs.

The formulas

term k = a · rk−1
sum = a(1 − rn+1) ÷ (1 − r), where rn+1 is the power on the rung after the last
forever sum = a ÷ (1 − r), only when r is between −1 and 1

Typing answers

Fractions like 5/12, powers like 2^10 or 2¹⁰, roots like √5 or sqrt(5). Both values: “a and b” or ±. A choice: one letter. A sequence: first term, ratio.
